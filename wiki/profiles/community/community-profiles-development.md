# Summary

A **Community Profile** is a baseline of CSF outcomes developed by or for a specific community of organizations sharing common mission goals, operational risks, or regulatory environments[^nist-csf-2-0].

# Examples
- NIST IR 8406: Cybersecurity Framework Profile for Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG).
- NIST IR 8441: Hybrid Satellite Networks Cybersecurity Profile.
- Election Infrastructure and Maritime Sector community profiles.
