# Summary

An **Organizational Profile** describes an organization's current and target cybersecurity posture in terms of CSF 2.0 Core outcomes[^nist-cswp-29].

# Operational Process
1. **Identify Requirements**: Synthesize business objectives, regulatory duties, threat intelligence, and risk appetite.
2. **Current State Profile**: Document which Core outcomes are currently achieved and how.
3. **Target State Profile**: Prioritize desired Core outcomes based on strategic risk reduction.
4. **Gap Analysis and Action Plan**: Track remediation initiatives to progress from Current to Target state.
