# Summary

**Tier 1 (Partial)** characterizes organizations where cybersecurity risk governance and risk management are ad hoc, reactive, and informal[^nist-cswp-29].

# CSF 2.0 Tier Dimensions (Table 2)

| Dimension | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Cybersecurity Risk Governance** | Organizational cybersecurity risk management practices are not formalized, and risk is managed in an ad hoc, sometimes reactive manner. Prioritization of cybersecurity activities is not informed by organizational risk objectives or threat environments. |
| **Cybersecurity Risk Management** | The organization has limited awareness of cybersecurity risks at the organizational level. Cybersecurity risk management occurs irregularly and without systemic process integration. |
