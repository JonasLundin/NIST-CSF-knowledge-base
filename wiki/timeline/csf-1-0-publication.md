# Summary

On **12 February 2014**, NIST published Version 1.0 of the Framework for Improving Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity in response to Presidential Executive Order 13636[^eo-13636].

# Milestone Details
- **Publication Date**: **12 February 2014**
- **Significance**: Established the initial CSF Core comprising Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover functions.
