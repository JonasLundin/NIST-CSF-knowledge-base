# Summary

On **12 February 2013**, President Barack Obama issued **Executive Order 13636: Improving Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity**, directing NIST to lead the development of a framework to reduce cyber risks to critical infrastructure[^eo-13636].

# Directive Details
- **Effective Date**: **12 February 2013**
- **Authority**: Binding Presidential Executive Order under federal administrative authority.
