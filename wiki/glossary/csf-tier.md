# Summary

In NIST CSF 2.0, a **CSF Tier** is a characterization of the rigor of an organization's cybersecurity risk governance and management practices[^nist-cswp-29].

# Definition (Appendix C)
Tiers characterize an organization's cybersecurity practices over a range from Partial (Tier 1) to Adaptive (Tier 4), reflecting a progression from informal, reactive responses to agile, risk-informed, and continuously improving approaches.
