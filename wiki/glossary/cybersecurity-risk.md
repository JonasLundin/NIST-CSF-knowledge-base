# Summary

**Cybersecurity Risk** is the effect of uncertainty on cybersecurity objectives, encompassing the potential for adverse outcomes arising from the exploitation of vulnerabilities in network and information systems[^nist-ir-8286].

# Definition (NIST IR 8286 & CSF 2.0 Appendix C)
An effect of uncertainty on cybersecurity objectives. Risk is expressed in terms of the likelihood and impact of threat events compromising confidentiality, integrity, or availability.
