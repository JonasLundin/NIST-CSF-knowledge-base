# Glossary

Terms as defined in CSWP 29 and NIST IR 8477.

## Concepts

- [Community Profile](community-profile.md) — A CSF profile tailored to a specific sector, technology, or community of organizations sharing common cybersecurity risks.
- [Cybersecurity Risk](cybersecurity-risk.md) — An effect of uncertainty on or within the information and communications technology environment.
- [Implementation Tier](implementation-tier.md) — A characterization of the rigor and sophistication of an organization's cybersecurity risk management practices.
- [Organizational Profile](organizational-profile.md) — A mechanism for describing an organization's current and target cybersecurity posture using CSF outcomes.
