# Glossary

Navigation index for Glossary.

## Concepts

- [CSF Community Profile](community-profile.md) — A CSF profile tailored to a specific sector, technology, or community of organizations sharing common cybersecurity risks.
- [CSF Category](csf-category.md) — A subdivision of a CSF Function into groups of cybersecurity outcomes closely tied to programmatic needs and particular activities.
- [CSF Core](csf-core.md) — A taxonomy of cybersecurity outcomes organized into Functions, Categories, and Subcategories.
- [CSF Subcategory](csf-subcategory.md) — A subdivision of a CSF Category into specific outcomes of technical or management activities.
- [CSF Tier](csf-tier.md) — Characterization of the rigor of an organization's cybersecurity risk governance and management practices across four levels.
- [Cybersecurity Risk](cybersecurity-risk.md) — An effect of uncertainty on or within the information and communications technology environment.
- [Informative Reference](informative-reference.md) — Specific sections of standards, guidelines, and practices common among critical infrastructure sectors that illustrate methods to achieve outcomes in each Subcategory.
- [CSF Organizational Profile](organizational-profile.md) — A mechanism for describing an organization's current and target cybersecurity posture using CSF outcomes.
