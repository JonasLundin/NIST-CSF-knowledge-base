# Crosswalks

This bundle's own mappings from subcategories to EU instruments, labelled inferred until an official mapping exists, using NIST IR 8477 relationship styles.

## Structure

- [Cyber Resilience Act](eu-cra/index.md): Subcategory to CRA Annex I and Article 13 and 14 relationships (pointers to CRA-knowledge-base).
- [NIS2](eu-nis2/index.md): Subcategory to NIS2 Article 21 measures and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 (pointers to NIS2-knowledge-base).
- [ISO/IEC 27001:2022](iso-iec-27001/index.md): Subcategory to clause and Annex A control identifiers only.
- [IEC 62443](iec-62443/index.md): Subcategory to IEC 62443 requirement identifiers only.
