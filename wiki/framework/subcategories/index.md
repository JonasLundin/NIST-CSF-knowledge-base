# CSF 2.0 Subcategories

The 106 Subcategories of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 Core organized across Functions and Categories.

> [!NOTE]
> **Numbering Gaps**: In CSF 2.0, certain numeric identifiers from CSF 1.1 were retired, merged, or moved (e.g. into the new GOVERN function), resulting in non-sequential numbering gaps within several Categories.

## Concepts

- [GV.OC-01](gv-oc-01.md) — The organizational mission is understood and informs cybersecurity risk management
- [GV.OC-02](gv-oc-02.md) — Internal and external stakeholders are understood, and their needs and expectations regarding cybersecurity risk management are understood and considered
- [GV.OC-03](gv-oc-03.md) — Legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements regarding cybersecurity - including privacy and civil liberties obligations - are understood and managed
- [GV.OC-04](gv-oc-04.md) — Critical objectives, capabilities, and services that stakeholders depend on or expect from the organization are understood and communicated
- [GV.OC-05](gv-oc-05.md) — Outcomes, capabilities, and services that the organization depends on are understood and communicated
- [GV.OV-01](gv-ov-01.md) — Cybersecurity risk management strategy outcomes are reviewed to inform and adjust strategy and direction
- [GV.OV-02](gv-ov-02.md) — The cybersecurity risk management strategy is reviewed and adjusted to ensure coverage of organizational requirements and risks
- [GV.OV-03](gv-ov-03.md) — Organizational cybersecurity risk management performance is evaluated and reviewed for adjustments needed
- [GV.PO-01](gv-po-01.md) — Policy for managing cybersecurity risks is established based on organizational context, cybersecurity strategy, and priorities and is communicated and enforced
- [GV.PO-02](gv-po-02.md) — Policy for managing cybersecurity risks is reviewed, updated, communicated, and enforced to reflect changes in requirements, threats, technology, and organizational mission
- [GV.RM-01](gv-rm-01.md) — Risk management objectives are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders
- [GV.RM-02](gv-rm-02.md) — Risk appetite and risk tolerance statements are established, communicated, and maintained
- [GV.RM-03](gv-rm-03.md) — Cybersecurity risk management activities and outcomes are included in enterprise risk management processes
- [GV.RM-04](gv-rm-04.md) — Strategic direction that describes appropriate risk response options is established and communicated
- [GV.RM-05](gv-rm-05.md) — Lines of communication across the organization are established for cybersecurity risks, including risks from suppliers and other third parties
- [GV.RM-06](gv-rm-06.md) — A standardized method for calculating, documenting, categorizing, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks is established and communicated
- [GV.RM-07](gv-rm-07.md) — Strategic opportunities (i.e., positive risks) are characterized and are included in organizational cybersecurity risk discussions
- [GV.RR-01](gv-rr-01.md) — Organizational leadership is responsible and accountable for cybersecurity risk and fosters a culture that is risk-aware, ethical, and continually improving
- [GV.RR-02](gv-rr-02.md) — Roles, responsibilities, and authorities related to cybersecurity risk management are established, communicated, understood, and enforced
- [GV.RR-03](gv-rr-03.md) — Adequate resources are allocated commensurate with the cybersecurity risk strategy, roles, responsibilities, and policies
- [GV.RR-04](gv-rr-04.md) — Cybersecurity is included in human resources practices
- [GV.SC-01](gv-sc-01.md) — A cybersecurity supply chain risk management program, strategy, objectives, policies, and processes are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders
- [GV.SC-02](gv-sc-02.md) — Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities for suppliers, customers, and partners are established, communicated, and coordinated internally and externally
- [GV.SC-03](gv-sc-03.md) — Cybersecurity supply chain risk management is integrated into cybersecurity and enterprise risk management, risk assessment, and improvement processes
- [GV.SC-04](gv-sc-04.md) — Suppliers are known and prioritized by criticality
- [GV.SC-05](gv-sc-05.md) — Requirements to address cybersecurity risks in supply chains are established, prioritized, and integrated into contracts and other types of agreements with suppliers and other relevant third parties
- [GV.SC-06](gv-sc-06.md) — Planning and due diligence are performed to reduce risks before entering into formal supplier or other third-party relationships
- [GV.SC-07](gv-sc-07.md) — The risks posed by a supplier, their products and services, and other third parties are understood, recorded, prioritized, assessed, responded to, and monitored over the course of the relationship
- [GV.SC-08](gv-sc-08.md) — Relevant suppliers and other third parties are included in incident planning, response, and recovery activities
- [GV.SC-09](gv-sc-09.md) — Supply chain security practices are integrated into cybersecurity and enterprise risk management programs, and their performance is monitored throughout the technology product and service life cycle
- [GV.SC-10](gv-sc-10.md) — Cybersecurity supply chain risk management plans include provisions for activities that occur after the conclusion of a partnership or service agreement
- [ID.AM-01](id-am-01.md) — Inventories of hardware managed by the organization are maintained
- [ID.AM-02](id-am-02.md) — Inventories of software, services, and systems managed by the organization are maintained
- [ID.AM-03](id-am-03.md) — Representations of the organization's authorized network communication and internal and external network data flows are maintained
- [ID.AM-04](id-am-04.md) — Inventories of services provided by suppliers are maintained
- [ID.AM-05](id-am-05.md) — Assets are prioritized based on classification, criticality, resources, and impact on the mission
- [ID.AM-07](id-am-07.md) — Inventories of data and corresponding metadata for designated data types are maintained
- [ID.AM-08](id-am-08.md) — Systems, hardware, software, services, and data are managed throughout their life cycles
- [ID.IM-01](id-im-01.md) — Improvements are identified from evaluations
- [ID.IM-02](id-im-02.md) — Improvements are identified from security tests and exercises, including those done in coordination with suppliers and relevant third parties
- [ID.IM-03](id-im-03.md) — Improvements are identified from execution of operational processes, procedures, and activities
- [ID.IM-04](id-im-04.md) — Incident response plans and other cybersecurity plans that affect operations are established, communicated, maintained, and improved
- [ID.RA-01](id-ra-01.md) — Vulnerabilities in assets are identified, validated, and recorded
- [ID.RA-02](id-ra-02.md) — Cyber threat intelligence is received from information sharing forums and sources
- [ID.RA-03](id-ra-03.md) — Internal and external threats to the organization are identified and recorded
- [ID.RA-04](id-ra-04.md) — Potential impacts and likelihoods of threats exploiting vulnerabilities are identified and recorded
- [ID.RA-05](id-ra-05.md) — Threats, vulnerabilities, likelihoods, and impacts are used to understand inherent risk and inform risk response prioritization
- [ID.RA-06](id-ra-06.md) — Risk responses are chosen, prioritized, planned, tracked, and communicated
- [ID.RA-07](id-ra-07.md) — Changes and exceptions are managed, assessed for risk impact, recorded, and tracked
- [ID.RA-08](id-ra-08.md) — Processes for receiving, analyzing, and responding to vulnerability disclosures are established
- [ID.RA-09](id-ra-09.md) — The authenticity and integrity of hardware and software are assessed prior to acquisition and use
- [ID.RA-10](id-ra-10.md) — Critical suppliers are assessed prior to acquisition
- [PR.AA-01](pr-aa-01.md) — Identities and credentials for authorized users, services, and hardware are managed by the organization
- [PR.AA-02](pr-aa-02.md) — Identities are proofed and bound to credentials based on the context of interactions
- [PR.AA-03](pr-aa-03.md) — Users, services, and hardware are authenticated
- [PR.AA-04](pr-aa-04.md) — Identity assertions are protected, conveyed, and verified
- [PR.AA-05](pr-aa-05.md) — Access permissions, entitlements, and authorizations are defined in a policy, managed, enforced, and reviewed, and incorporate the principles of least privilege and separation of duties
- [PR.AA-06](pr-aa-06.md) — Physical access to assets is managed, monitored, and enforced commensurate with risk
- [PR.AT-01](pr-at-01.md) — Personnel are provided with awareness and training so that they possess the knowledge and skills to perform general tasks with cybersecurity risks in mind
- [PR.AT-02](pr-at-02.md) — Individuals in specialized roles are provided with awareness and training so that they possess the knowledge and skills to perform relevant tasks with cybersecurity risks in mind
- [PR.DS-01](pr-ds-01.md) — The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-at-rest are protected
- [PR.DS-02](pr-ds-02.md) — The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-in-transit are protected
- [PR.DS-10](pr-ds-10.md) — The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-in-use are protected
- [PR.DS-11](pr-ds-11.md) — Backups of data are created, protected, maintained, and tested
- [PR.IR-01](pr-ir-01.md) — Networks and environments are protected from unauthorized logical access and usage
- [PR.IR-02](pr-ir-02.md) — The organization's technology assets are protected from environmental threats
- [PR.IR-03](pr-ir-03.md) — Mechanisms are implemented to achieve resilience requirements in normal and adverse situations
- [PR.IR-04](pr-ir-04.md) — Adequate resource capacity to ensure availability is maintained
- [PR.PS-01](pr-ps-01.md) — Configuration management practices are established and applied
- [PR.PS-02](pr-ps-02.md) — Software is maintained, replaced, and removed commensurate with risk
- [PR.PS-03](pr-ps-03.md) — Hardware is maintained, replaced, and removed commensurate with risk
- [PR.PS-04](pr-ps-04.md) — Log records are generated and made available for continuous monitoring
- [PR.PS-05](pr-ps-05.md) — Installation and execution of unauthorized software are prevented
- [PR.PS-06](pr-ps-06.md) — Secure software development practices are integrated, and their performance is monitored throughout the software development life cycle
- [DE.AE-02](de-ae-02.md) — Potentially adverse events are analyzed to better understand associated activities
- [DE.AE-03](de-ae-03.md) — Information is correlated from multiple sources
- [DE.AE-04](de-ae-04.md) — The estimated impact and scope of adverse events are understood
- [DE.AE-06](de-ae-06.md) — Information on adverse events is provided to authorized staff and tools
- [DE.AE-07](de-ae-07.md) — Cyber threat intelligence and other contextual information are integrated into the analysis
- [DE.AE-08](de-ae-08.md) — Incidents are declared when adverse events meet the defined incident criteria
- [DE.CM-01](de-cm-01.md) — Networks and network services are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- [DE.CM-02](de-cm-02.md) — The physical environment is monitored to find potentially adverse events
- [DE.CM-03](de-cm-03.md) — Personnel activity and technology usage are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- [DE.CM-06](de-cm-06.md) — External service provider activities and services are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- [DE.CM-09](de-cm-09.md) — Computing hardware and software, runtime environments, and their data are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- [RS.AN-03](rs-an-03.md) — Analysis is performed to establish what has taken place during an incident and the root cause of the incident
- [RS.AN-06](rs-an-06.md) — Actions performed during an investigation are recorded, and the records' integrity and provenance are preserved
- [RS.AN-07](rs-an-07.md) — Incident data and metadata are collected, and their integrity and provenance are preserved
- [RS.AN-08](rs-an-08.md) — An incident's magnitude is estimated and validated
- [RS.CO-02](rs-co-02.md) — Internal and external stakeholders are notified of incidents
- [RS.CO-03](rs-co-03.md) — Information is shared with designated internal and external stakeholders
- [RS.MA-01](rs-ma-01.md) — The incident response plan is executed in coordination with relevant third parties once an incident is declared
- [RS.MA-02](rs-ma-02.md) — Incident reports are triaged and validated
- [RS.MA-03](rs-ma-03.md) — Incidents are categorized and prioritized
- [RS.MA-04](rs-ma-04.md) — Incidents are escalated or elevated as needed
- [RS.MA-05](rs-ma-05.md) — The criteria for initiating incident recovery are applied
- [RS.MI-01](rs-mi-01.md) — Incidents are contained
- [RS.MI-02](rs-mi-02.md) — Incidents are eradicated
- [RC.CO-03](rc-co-03.md) — Recovery activities and progress in restoring operational capabilities are communicated to designated internal and external stakeholders
- [RC.CO-04](rc-co-04.md) — Public updates on incident recovery are shared using approved methods and messaging
- [RC.RP-01](rc-rp-01.md) — The recovery portion of the incident response plan is executed once initiated from the incident response process
- [RC.RP-02](rc-rp-02.md) — Recovery actions are selected, scoped, prioritized, and performed
- [RC.RP-03](rc-rp-03.md) — The integrity of backups and other restoration assets is verified before using them for restoration
- [RC.RP-04](rc-rp-04.md) — Critical mission functions and cybersecurity risk management are considered to establish post-incident operational norms
- [RC.RP-05](rc-rp-05.md) — The integrity of restored assets is verified, systems and services are restored, and normal operating status is confirmed
- [RC.RP-06](rc-rp-06.md) — The end of incident recovery is declared based on criteria, and incident-related documentation is completed
