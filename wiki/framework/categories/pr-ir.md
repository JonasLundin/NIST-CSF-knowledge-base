# Summary

Category **PR.IR (Technology Infrastructure Resilience)** within the [PR (Protect)](../functions/protect.md) Function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0[^nist-cswp-29].

Security architectures are managed with the organization's risk strategy to protect asset confidentiality, integrity, and availability, and organizational resilience

# Scope

This category groups related cybersecurity outcomes that support the Protect function.

# Subcategories

Subcategories under PR.IR:
- [PR.IR-01](../subcategories/pr-ir-01.md): Networks and environments are protected from unauthorized logical access and usage
- [PR.IR-02](../subcategories/pr-ir-02.md): The organization's technology assets are protected from environmental threats
- [PR.IR-03](../subcategories/pr-ir-03.md): Mechanisms are implemented to achieve resilience requirements in normal and adverse situations
- [PR.IR-04](../subcategories/pr-ir-04.md): Adequate resource capacity to ensure availability is maintained
