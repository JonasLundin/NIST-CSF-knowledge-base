# Summary

Category **GV.RR (Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities)** within the [GV (Govern)](../functions/govern.md) Function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0[^nist-cswp-29].

Cybersecurity roles, responsibilities, and authorities to foster accountability, performance assessment, and continuous improvement are established and communicated

# Scope

This category groups related cybersecurity outcomes that support the Govern function.

# Subcategories

Subcategories under GV.RR:
- [GV.RR-01](../subcategories/gv-rr-01.md): Organizational leadership is responsible and accountable for cybersecurity risk and fosters a culture that is risk-aware, ethical, and continually improving
- [GV.RR-02](../subcategories/gv-rr-02.md): Roles, responsibilities, and authorities related to cybersecurity risk management are established, communicated, understood, and enforced
- [GV.RR-03](../subcategories/gv-rr-03.md): Adequate resources are allocated commensurate with the cybersecurity risk strategy, roles, responsibilities, and policies
- [GV.RR-04](../subcategories/gv-rr-04.md): Cybersecurity is included in human resources practices
