# Summary

Category **ID.IM (Improvement)** within the [ID (Identify)](../functions/identify.md) Function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0[^nist-cswp-29].

Improvements to organizational cybersecurity risk management processes, procedures and activities are identified across all CSF Functions

# Scope

This category groups related cybersecurity outcomes that support the Identify function.

Subcategories under ID.IM:

- [ID.IM-03](../subcategories/id-im-03.md): Improvements are identified from execution of operational processes, procedures, and activities
