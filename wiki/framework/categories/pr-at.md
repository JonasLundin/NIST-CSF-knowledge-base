# Summary

Category **PR.AT (Awareness and Training)** within the [PR (Protect)](../functions/protect.md) Function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0[^nist-cswp-29].

The organization's personnel are provided with cybersecurity awareness and training so that they can perform their cybersecurity-related tasks

# Scope

This category groups related cybersecurity outcomes that support the Protect function.

# Subcategories

Subcategories under PR.AT:
- [PR.AT-01](../subcategories/pr-at-01.md): Personnel are provided with awareness and training so that they possess the knowledge and skills to perform general tasks with cybersecurity risks in mind
- [PR.AT-02](../subcategories/pr-at-02.md): Individuals in specialized roles are provided with awareness and training so that they possess the knowledge and skills to perform relevant tasks with cybersecurity risks in mind
