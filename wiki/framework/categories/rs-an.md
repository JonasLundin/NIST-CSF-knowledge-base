# Summary

Category **RS.AN (Incident Analysis)** within the [RS (Respond)](../functions/respond.md) Function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0[^nist-cswp-29].

Investigations are conducted to ensure effective response and support forensics and recovery activities

# Scope

This category groups related cybersecurity outcomes that support the Respond function.

Subcategories under RS.AN:
