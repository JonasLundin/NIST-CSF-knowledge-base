# Functions

Navigation index for Functions.

## Concepts

- [DE: Detect](detect.md) — Possible cybersecurity attacks and compromises are found and analyzed.
- [Function: Govern (GV)](govern.md) — The organization's cybersecurity risk management strategy, expectations, and policy are established, communicated, and monitored.
- [ID: Identify](identify.md) — The organization's current cybersecurity risks are understood.
- [PR: Protect](protect.md) — Safeguards to manage the organization's cybersecurity risks are used.
- [RC: Recover](recover.md) — Assets and operations affected by a cybersecurity incident are restored.
- [RS: Respond](respond.md) — Actions regarding a detected cybersecurity incident are taken.
